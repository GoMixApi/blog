Dola Seedance 2.5: ByteDance's Pro Video Model — What You Need to Know

Published: 2026-08-09 · Category: Industry

ByteDance just dropped Dola Seedance 2.5 — a professional-grade multimodal video creation model. 30-second direct output. Up to 50 reference assets. 10+ languages. Here's what it means for developers building in Southeast Asia.

What Is Dola Seedance 2.5?

Seedance 2.5 is ByteDance's latest video generation model under the Dola product line. It's not just a text-to-video tool — it's a full video creation suite:

  • Generate videos from reference images, existing videos, and audio
  • Edit videos with precise instruction control
  • Extend videos from existing clips
  • Frame-to-frame generation with start and end frame control

Pricing (Reference)

ByteDance prices video generation by token consumption, with two tiers:

  • 720p (Recommended): $0.85/sec or $24.9/30s standard clip (GoMixAPI estimated, final price subject to change)
  • 480p: $0.52/sec or $15.4/clip · 1080p: Coming soon

Note: Token consumption varies significantly by resolution and duration. Higher resolution = more tokens per video.

Why This Matters for Southeast Asia

1. Multilingual Native Support

10+ languages out of the box — this is critical for SEA markets where content needs to work across Bahasa, Thai, Vietnamese, Tagalog, and more.

2. Singapore Node Advantage

ByteDance's BytePlus ARK already has Singapore infrastructure. Video generation latency could be significantly lower for SEA developers compared to Runway or Pika, which route through US/EU.

3. E-Commerce & Creator Economy

SEA's booming e-commerce and creator economy need affordable, scalable video production. A model that can ingest product images and output 30-second product videos — in local languages — is a direct fit for Shopee/Lazada/TikTok sellers.
