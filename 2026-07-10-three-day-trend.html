🔍 Key Insights After 3 Days

1. DeepSeek V4 Flash is the Most Consistent

Three days of testing and DeepSeek V4 Flash consistently delivers the fastest results. Code generation speed stayed in the 3.7-4.0s range — impressive stability for a model handling 1M context windows.

2. Hy-MT2-Plus Dominates Translation

Introduced on Day 2, Hy-MT2-Plus immediately took the translation crown at 0.91s — 2x faster than DeepSeek and 10x faster than Qwen. At $0.15/$0.90 per 1M tokens, it's also one of the most affordable models.

3. Qwen3.7-Plus: Slow But Thorough

Qwen consistently takes the longest but produces the most detailed outputs. Its code generation output averaged 750+ chars vs DeepSeek's 400. For use cases valuing thoroughness over speed, Qwen is the right choice.

4. Day-to-Day Variance is Low

Speed variation between days is under 10% for most tasks. This suggests stable infrastructure and consistent model performance — good news for production workloads.
