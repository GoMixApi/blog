Key Takeaways After One Full Week

1. Zero Failures Across 105 Tests

Not a single timeout. Not a single 500 error. Not a single malformed response. This level of infrastructure reliability across seven consecutive days is what enterprises should expect before moving workloads to production.

2. DeepSeek V4 Flash is the Clear Production Choice

Fastest average speed. Lowest variance. Zero failures. At $0.10/$0.45 per 1M tokens, it is both the most predictable and most affordable model in our benchmark. For any team deploying AI into production, this is where you should start.

3. Specialized Models Earn Their Place

Hy-MT2-Plus is not the best at code generation. But at translation, it is 2-10x faster than every other model. Qwen3.7-Plus is slow but produces the most thorough outputs. The lesson: use the right model for the right task.

4. Day-to-Day Variance is Under 10%

For models in production, predictability matters as much as peak performance. Over seven days, speed variance across all models stayed within a tight band. This is evidence of stable infrastructure at every layer.
