Price Breakdown: Counting Every Penny

DeepSeek is the cost killer. If your workload is massive-scale text processing or batch inference, nothing beats the $0.10/$0.45 per million tokens of DeepSeek V4 Flash. It's the sharpest "traffic spike" in your pricing arsenal.

Dola's premium buys you more than a name. Both Dola Seed models handle multimodal inputs (audio, images) natively, and they run on BytePlus's Singapore nodes — the same region as your Southeast Asian users. For real-time consumer apps, that low latency is a conversion feature, not just a number.

Benchmarks That Matter: Coding & Speed

Coding Head-to-Head

We run the same coding task every day. DeepSeek V4 Pro remains the coding king in our benchmark suite (average 6.22s), but Dola Seed-2.1-turbo has closed the gap significantly (46.02s). For most real-world API endpoints, you won't feel a difference — the speed advantage matters more.

The Real Differentiator: Dola's SG-Native Speed

In our daily translation task, Dola models respond in 21.48s (turbo) vs 8.72s (Flash). When your users are in Jakarta, Bangkok, or Singapore, every millisecond counts. Dola's Singapore-native deployment gives you the edge in perceived performance.
