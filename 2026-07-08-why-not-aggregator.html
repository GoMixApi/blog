我们为什么不做“聚合平台”

Published: 2026-07-08 · Category: Product · Author: GoMixAPI Team

The term "AI aggregator" is becoming a liability.

It carries a set of assumptions that are hard to shake: middleman. Reseller. Replaceable. Competing on price. When developers hear "aggregator," their first thought is often: "Oh, another platform reselling tokens."

That's not how we see ourselves.

On the surface, GoMixAPI connects DeepSeek, Qwen, GLM, Hunyuan, and Dola — China's leading AI models — to developers across Southeast Asia through a single OpenAI-compatible endpoint. But what we actually sell isn't model forwarding. It's enterprise-grade AI access capability.

What does that mean?

It means a DPA and SCC that your legal team can actually sign.
It means one unified invoice instead of juggling five separate upstream accounts.
It means a zero-retention architecture where your prompts and outputs are discarded in real-time, never persisted to disk.
It means fully auditable logs — every token consumed, every charge applied, every model called — exportable for your own audit trail.

These are things an "aggregator" doesn't do, and doesn't need to do. Only a team that thinks of itself as infrastructure would build compliance, transparency, and data sovereignty into the product from day one — before pricing, before model count, before anything else.

That's why we're doing a few things that aggregators typically don't:

  • Public Benchmark Data: Every day, we automatically test DeepSeek, Qwen, GLM, Dola, and Hunyuan on code generation, translation, and long-context summarization. All results are published on GitHub.
  • Price Watch Reports: We track upstream model pricing changes weekly and publish them publicly.
  • Live Status Page: Real-time model availability and latency data, publicly accessible.
  • Transparent Pricing Logic: Our How We Price page explains the billing formula, what our margin covers, and our full price change history.

None of this is marketing. It's the operational reality of being infrastructure. The value of infrastructure isn't measured in "how much cheaper per token" — it's measured in whether someone trusts you enough to put their production workload on your platform.

So we don't call ourselves an aggregator. We are an Enterprise AI Gateway — infrastructure for Southeast Asian businesses to access China's leading AI models.

That positioning comes with a heavier responsibility than "aggregation." It requires deeper trust. It's a harder path. But once you build it, no one can replace you.
