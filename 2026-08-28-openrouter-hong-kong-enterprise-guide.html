The Enterprise Decision Framework

Most technical evaluations focus on model coverage, but production deployments fail for non-technical reasons. Here is the framework we recommend:

  1. Compliance & Legal: Who is the contracting entity? Can they sign a DPA? Are SCCs available?
  2. Performance & Latency: Where are the inference nodes? What is the expected TTFB for your users?
  3. Data Privacy & Retention: Are prompts and completions logged? How long are they retained?
  4. Cost Predictability: Are prices transparent? Is there volume discounting?
  5. Support & SLA: What happens when something breaks at 3 AM Hong Kong time?
